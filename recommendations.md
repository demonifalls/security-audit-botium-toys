# Security Recommendations

Based on the security audit, Botium Toys should prioritize implementing least privilege and separation of duties to restrict access to sensitive customer and cardholder information.

The organization should also implement encryption for sensitive data, strengthen password requirements through a centralized password management system, deploy an intrusion detection system, and establish regular backups and a disaster recovery plan.

Additionally, Botium Toys should improve asset and data classification practices and continue addressing PCI DSS and GDPR compliance gaps.

These improvements would reduce the risk of unauthorized access, data loss, and regulatory penalties while strengthening the organization's overall security posture.
